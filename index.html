How a project works

Clear scope up front, steady progress you can see, and work you can keep running after I hand it off.

  1. Talk it through

    Email me what is going on. We set up a short call to understand the problem, your tools, and what a good outcome looks like.

  2. Agree on scope

    You get a written statement of work with deliverables, timeline, and price, so there are no surprises.

  3. Build and check in

    I work asynchronously and send regular written updates, with live check-ins scheduled when they are useful.

  4. Hand off

    Documentation and a walkthrough, so your team understands what was built and can keep it running.

Common questions

Do you work with small companies?

Yes. Many of the best projects are at companies with no data team yet, where one person cleaning up the foundations makes a big difference. I scope work to fit your budget.

Can you work with the tools we already have?

Usually, yes. I would rather improve what you have than sell you a new stack. If a tool is genuinely holding you back, I will explain why and what it would cost to change.

How do you handle our data and access?

I sign an NDA before seeing anything sensitive, ask only for the access a project needs, and work inside your systems wherever possible rather than copying data out.

How is pricing set?

Most work is a fixed price per project, agreed before it starts. Ongoing help is a monthly retainer. Send me a short description and I will reply with an approach and an estimate.

Have a data or AI problem worth solving?

Send a few lines about your business, the tools you use, and what you are trying to figure out. I usually reply within one business day.
